# ProjectHub Community Guidelines

ProjectHub is designed to help students find teammates and collaborate on projects.

We expect users to:

- Treat other users respectfully.
- Provide truthful profile and project information.
- Respect intellectual-property rights.
- Keep project communication relevant and professional.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination or abuse.
- Do not share another person's private information without permission.
- Do not upload malware, scams or deceptive content.
- Do not misuse Project AI or the platform for unlawful activity.
- Report security or serious abuse concerns to an appropriate ProjectHub contact.

Violations may result in content removal, account restrictions or account termination where appropriate.
