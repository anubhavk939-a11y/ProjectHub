# ProjectHub Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 27, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 27, 2026

ProjectHub ("ProjectHub", "we", "us", or "our") is a student collaboration platform created and operated by Kumar Anubhava.

**Privacy contact:** anubhavk939@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what information ProjectHub may collect, how it is used, how it may be shared with service providers, and the choices available to users of the ProjectHub website and Android application.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ProjectHub, we may collect:

### Account information
- Name
- Email address
- College/institution information
- Password information in securely hashed form
- Profile information that you choose to provide
- Skills and professional links such as GitHub or LinkedIn URLs

### Project and collaboration information
- Projects you create
- Project titles, descriptions, categories and required skills
- Applications to join projects
- Application messages and statuses
- Messages sent within project workspaces

### AI interaction information
When you use Project AI, we may process the question you submit and relevant project context needed to generate a response. Project AI currently uses Google Gemini through the Google Gemini API.

Do not submit passwords, payment card information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information to Project AI.

### Technical information
Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device information, request logs, and security-related information as necessary to operate and protect the service.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:
- Create and authenticate accounts
- Provide profiles, projects, applications and collaboration features
- Display project and profile information to other ProjectHub users as intended by the service
- Provide project messaging
- Provide Project AI responses
- Maintain, secure and troubleshoot the service
- Prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access and other security issues
- Communicate with users about service or support matters
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

## 3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information as a business model.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate ProjectHub, including:
- Hosting and deployment providers such as Vercel and Render
- Database/infrastructure providers used by the ProjectHub service
- Google Gemini, when a user uses Project AI and information is necessary to generate the requested response
- Other providers we may use for security, infrastructure or service operations

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce our terms, protect users or the service, or respond to valid legal requests.

## 4. Public and User-Visible Information

ProjectHub is a collaboration platform. Information you choose to put in your profile or project may be visible to other users depending on the feature.

Do not publish information you do not want other ProjectHub users to see.

## 5. Passwords and Security

Passwords are not intended to be stored in plain text. ProjectHub uses password hashing for account authentication.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide ProjectHub, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal obligations.

When an account and associated data are deleted, we intend to remove associated user data from active systems, subject to information that may need to be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.
