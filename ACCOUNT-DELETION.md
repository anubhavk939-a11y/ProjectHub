# ProjectHub Account & Data Deletion

ProjectHub allows users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

## Request deletion

Send an email to:

**anubhavk939@gmail.com**

Use the subject:

**ProjectHub Account Deletion Request**

Include the email address associated with your ProjectHub account.

We may request reasonable information to verify ownership of the account before processing the request.

## What we intend to delete

Subject to legal, security and fraud-prevention requirements, account deletion may include:
- Account profile information
- Authentication information associated with the account
- Projects owned by the account, where deletion is technically and legally appropriate
- Applications associated with the account
- Messages and other user-associated information

Some information may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law, security requirements, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or other legitimate purposes.

## Processing

Deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after verification.

For Google Play distribution, the Android application must also provide an in-app way to initiate account deletion. This document provides the external/web request path.
