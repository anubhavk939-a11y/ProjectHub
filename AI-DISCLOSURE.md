# ProjectHub AI Disclosure

ProjectHub includes an optional feature called **Project AI**.

## What Project AI does

Project AI can help users with project-related questions such as:
- Project roadmaps
- Feature ideas
- Technology choices
- Task division
- Project presentations
- General project planning

## AI provider

Project AI currently uses **Google Gemini** through the Google Gemini API.

When a user submits a question to Project AI, ProjectHub may send the question together with relevant project context required to generate the response.

## Important limitations

AI-generated responses may be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated or inappropriate for a particular situation.

Users should independently review important AI-generated information before relying on it.

Do not submit passwords, payment details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information to Project AI.
